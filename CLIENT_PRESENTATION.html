Healthcare Staffing Management System

Complete Workflow Overview

End-to-End Staff Management Solution

1
Staff Registration

New healthcare staff (Doctors/Nurses) register through the mobile application.

Registration includes:

  • Personal information (name, email, phone)
  • Professional credentials (license number, specialization)
  • Department and location preferences
  • Emergency contact information
2
Job Creation by Admin/HR

Admin or HR personnel create job postings through the system.

Job details include:

  • Job title, department, and specialization requirements
  • Date and time schedule (start/end dates, shift times)
  • Location and facility details
  • Hourly rate and priority level
  • Required qualifications
3
Staff Job Acceptance

Registered staff members can view available jobs matching their profile.

Staff can browse and filter jobs by:

  • Department and Location
  • Date range and Rate range

Staff members accept jobs that match their availability. Multiple staff can accept the same job posting.

4
HR Candidate Selection

HR/Admin reviews all candidates who accepted the job.

HR can view candidate profiles including:

  • Qualifications and experience
  • Specialization and department match
  • Previous performance history

HR selects the most suitable candidate. Selected candidate is confirmed, and others are automatically notified.

5
Check-In Process

On the scheduled day, staff member arrives at the facility.

Two check-in methods available:

  • Manual Check-In: Staff manually confirms location using GPS
  • QR Code Check-In: Staff scans a QR code generated by the system

Location is automatically captured and verified.

6
QR Code Generation & Scanning

System generates a unique QR code for each job assignment.

QR code contains:

  • Assignment ID and Job details
  • Facility location and Time stamp

Staff scans QR code using mobile app camera. Location is automatically captured via GPS. Check-in data is submitted for approval.

7
HR Approval of Check-In

HR/Admin receives notification of pending check-in.

HR reviews the check-in details:

  • Verification of location (GPS coordinates)
  • Time verification
  • Staff member identity

HR approves or rejects the check-in. Staff receives confirmation. Job status changes to "IN_PROGRESS".

8
Check-Out Process

At the end of the shift, staff member checks out.

Check-out includes:

  • Location verification (GPS)
  • Time stamp
  • Shift completion confirmation

Staff can check-out via manual or QR code scanning. Job status updates to "COMPLETED".

9
Reports & Analytics

Comprehensive reporting system available for HR/Admin.

Reports include:

  • Job Reports: All jobs with status and completion rates
  • Assignment Reports: Staff assignments with check-in/out times
  • Check-In/Out Reports: Detailed attendance tracking
  • Staff Performance: Individual activity and job history

Reports can be filtered and exported to PDF/Excel formats.
